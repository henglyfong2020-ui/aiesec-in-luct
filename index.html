About Us

Who we are.

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organization, active in over 100 countries. AIESEC in LUCT is our local committee — a community of students at the Limkokwing University of Creative Technology building leadership through action.

Vision

Peace and fulfilment of humankind's potential.

Mission

To develop the leadership of every young person we engage.

Why LUCT?

A creative, international campus that mirrors the world our members go on to lead in.
