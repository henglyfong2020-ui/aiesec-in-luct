About Us

Who we are.

AIESEC in LUCT is a youth-led community created in 2009 where young people come together to develop leadership, create meaningful experiences, and make an impact. Through challenges, opportunities, and shared experiences, we empower our members to discover their potential and grow beyond their comfort zones.

Impact Story

The Challenge → The Impact

Impact Day 2026 with AIESEC in LUCT was a rewarding experience that brought our team to Prey Chum Orphanage Center in Kampong Speu. With 80 children, we focused on promoting essential health and hygiene habits while encouraging our youth delegates to prioritize self-care.

Beyond health education, the event highlighted the center’s urgent needs for food, hygiene supplies, and specialized equipment, including a wheelchair for children with limited mobility. The experience reminded us that creating impact goes beyond a single event—it requires continued commitment, advocacy, and action to support our communities. 💚🍀

Past Events

Tech Talk

TECH TALK 2026 empowers youth, students, entrepreneurs, and young professionals through technology, innovation, and future-ready skills. Through expert talks, workshops, challenges, and mentorship, participants gain practical skills while turning ideas into solutions for real-world challenges.
Aligned with SDG #8: Decent Work and Economic Growth, the event connects young people with industry leaders and partners to strengthen employability, innovation, and entrepreneurship while contributing to Cambodia’s development.

Impact Day

Impact Day 2026 is a charity event promoting good health and well-being through regular physical exercise and proper hygiene, aligned with SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being.
The event raises awareness of the importance of an active lifestyle and good hygiene in maintaining physical and mental health. Through engaging activities and educational sessions, participants will learn practical healthy habits while fostering responsibility, discipline, and a supportive community.

Skill Forge

SKILLS FORGE is an interactive event centered on “Public Relations 101: Etiquette and Organizational Values Alignment.” It aims to equip youth, university students, and young professionals with essential PR and professional etiquette skills.
Through expert speaker sessions and engaging activities, participants will learn about message crafting, audience awareness, stakeholder engagement, and effective workplace collaboration. The event also emphasizes ethical and consistent brand representation when interacting with external stakeholders. Aligned with SDG #8: Decent Work and Economic Growth.

Questions, answered.

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organization, present in over 100 countries, focused on developing leadership through international exchange and hands-on experience.

Any current LUCT student, regardless of major or year, can apply to become a member.

No. Most members join with zero leadership experience — that's exactly what AIESEC is built to develop.

Join AIESEC member is free. Full details are shared during the application process.

AIESEC in LUCT primarily welcome our Local Committee University Market; students from other universities can apply as well.

Apply through our Exchange page or EXPA, get matched to a program, and our Exchange team will guide you through preparation and departure.

Programs typically run from six weeks to several months, depending on the program and host opportunity.

Conversational English is generally sufficient, although specific language requirements may vary depending on the host opportunity. Even if you are not fully fluent in English, we still encourage you to apply, as language skills can be developed throughout the experience.
